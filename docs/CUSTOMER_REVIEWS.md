# Customer reviews

Customer page: https://maxvolumenutriton.shop/reviews.html

In Admin → Reviews → Google review link, completed purchases & review requests:

1. From the **Kelambakkam Max Volume Nutrition** Google Business Profile, open Read reviews → Get more reviews and copy the direct review link. Check its destination, paste it into the settings and confirm the business identity. Until saved, the customer page explains that the Google link is awaiting confirmation. A Maps search URL and an unconfirmed older office profile are not substitutes.
2. Copy the neutral request draft or download the QR. Offer the same request to every genuine customer after receipt and enough time to try the product. Send manually in an appropriate customer conversation. No messages are sent by this feature. No incentives, positive-rating requests or satisfaction filtering.
3. For WhatsApp/offline sales, check the actual receipt and completed delivery. Match the customer's registered account email and product, record the reference and received date, and confirm. This controls only the verified purchase badge. Revoke incorrect verification using the corresponding button. Do not record an enquiry as a delivered purchase. Website paid and delivered orders qualify automatically for their account and purchased product.
4. Review pending feedback without regard to rating. Publish authentic positive and critical feedback. A rejection requires a content reason: spam, no product experience, abuse, personal information or duplicate. The reason and administrator are audited. Customers see the reason on their own product review form.

Registered customers affirm actual product experience. One review per product/account is enforced by SQLite. Edits return to pending; customers can delete their own reviews. Public content shows first name, rating, date and a verified/unverified purchase label. Receipt references, email and account IDs stay private. Registration and experience affirmation alone do not prove a purchase. Human moderation is still required to check authenticity; the system does not guarantee every submission is genuine.

Only published reviews contribute to a product's visible score and its server-rendered structured data. Zero reviews means no AggregateRating. Google service reviews are separate and are never imported into product scores. Search stars are not guaranteed.

Storage changes are additive (`review_purchases` and `review_moderation` in the existing SQLite database); the existing backup system includes them. This setup does not enable payment, shipping or automatic marketing, provision services, or post reviews to Google. The review QR targets the stable customer hub, so it does not need reprinting after the Google link is saved.

Google references: https://support.google.com/business/answer/16816815 and https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/review-snippet
