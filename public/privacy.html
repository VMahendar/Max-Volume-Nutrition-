Privacy policy

Draft for owner review. Online checkout stays disabled until the store approves its policies and business details.

Information used for an order

The WhatsApp order form asks for your name, phone number, delivery address, city, state, PIN code and payment preference. Submitting it opens WhatsApp with those details and your selected products. You choose whether to send that message to our team. The cart is stored in your browser. The store does not write delivery details or payment preferences to localStorage. Your browser and WhatsApp may retain the prefilled request.

Recently viewed products

The store remembers up to four recently viewed product identifiers in this browser. This history is not sent to our server. Use Clear history in the Recently viewed section to remove it, or clear your browser storage.

Payments

The current storefront arranges payment directly with our team on WhatsApp. Never send card details, UPI PINs or OTPs in chat. When enabled, online payment credentials are entered through Razorpay Checkout. The store backend retains the order reference, amount and verification status; it does not store card numbers, UPI PINs or OTPs.

Service providers

Payment details are handled by the payment provider. Delivery information may be shared with a courier to fulfil an order. Manufacturer reference images and Google Fonts may make requests to external providers.

Optional messages and reminders

Registered customers may explicitly choose replenishment reminders every 15 days or 30 days, plus optional festival and product-launch announcements. The opt-in choices also specify email or WhatsApp. We store those preferences, the provided contact number if selected, and the dates of consent and manual follow-up. No promotional messages are sent by the website automatically while a sending service has not been configured and approved. You can change preferences or stop all marketing updates anytime through My Account. We do not automatically enrol past buyers.
