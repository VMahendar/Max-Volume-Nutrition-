Our review policy

  • Optional WhatsApp and SMS review requests require separate permission after a completed purchase. Use the stop link in your invitation or your account preferences to withdraw it. Google review submission cannot be tracked automatically; you can dismiss reminders after reviewing.
  • All ratings from 1 to 5 stars are welcome. We do not offer rewards for reviews or ask only happy customers to review us.
  • Describe your own experience. Do not post advertising, copied reviews, abuse, private contact details or someone else's experience.
  • Product reviews are checked for these content rules. A low rating or criticism is not a reason to reject a review. Google reviews follow Google's separate policies.
  • “Verified purchase” means a paid and delivered website order, or a completed sale checked by our team, is matched to the signed-in account and product. Otherwise the review says “Purchase not verified”. An enquiry alone does not qualify.
  • Your first name, review, rating and date are public. Account email and receipt references remain private. Sign in on the product page to update or delete your review. Edits are checked again before publication.
  • Product scores include all published ratings, including low ratings. Google service reviews are not mixed into product scores. No score is displayed until reviews exist.

Questions about moderation? Contact our team.
